BOUNCE!

Instructions

Arrow keys -> add energy in a direction to all balls
Shift keys -> subtract energy from all balls


COMMAND LINE INPUTS

./bounce NUMBER_OF_BALLS RADIUS GRAVITY

Ex: ./bounce 1000 0.5 -9.81
This summons 1000 balls 0.5m radius with -9.81m/ss in Y-Direction

Note: Brownian motion can be observed at low energy!
	 Ball color indicates energy level.
